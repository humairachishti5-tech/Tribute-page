About Allama Iqbal

Allama Muhammad Iqbal was a famous philosopher, poet, and thinker of the Indian subcontinent. His poetry encouraged people to develop confidence, knowledge, and a strong sense of purpose.

Iqbal was born in Sialkot in 1877. He received his early education in Sialkot and later continued his studies in Lahore and Europe. His education and philosophical ideas strongly influenced his literary work.

His poetry was written mainly in Urdu and Persian. Through his writings, he inspired people to believe in themselves and work toward a better future.

Allama Iqbal is remembered as one of the most influential thinkers and poets of South Asian history. His ideas continue to be studied and appreciated today.
